# Chapter 1 v4

`original.html` is the supplied prototype. Its embedded atlases include the bowl, ingredients, steam and effects, so no sprite-sheet substitution or re-cropping is needed. The packaging script retains its animation sequence, corrects the full bowl height by dividing its aspect by 1.15, and keeps ingredient rows vertically centred while visible.

The app supplies the Figma burgundy background and shared upper-layer carpet corners. Progress, count, click and keyboard controls from the prototype are hidden or disabled on the audience display. Setup provides lift, separate, list and return controls. Selecting a chapter shows a waiting screen; an upward hand sweep starts the intro.

On return, ingredient thumbnails retain their list sizes and converge into several nearby positions above the bowl centre. A short hold shows the assembled ingredients before the hero crossfade.
