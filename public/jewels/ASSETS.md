# Chapter 05 · Jewels of the Empire

`original.html` archives the supplied Chapter 5 prototype. The packaging script keeps its orbit, copy and glints, with a group entrance and an exit that drops only the currently displayed item. Full-screen glitter complements the orbit particles. Commands are provided through Setup; audience keyboard interaction is disabled.

`silhouette-trio.png` is copied unchanged from the supplied Mughal Jewelry Showcase Trio. Texture UVs select its left third for the hand and right-hand head region (starting at source x=1568 to exclude the neighbouring bust), with their effective aspect ratios preserved. `shoulder-silhouette.png` is the supplied individual Ornate Indian Gold Collar with Jewelled Epaulettes. Original alpha is preserved. `background.png` is the supplied burgundy fallback image, explicitly labelled NOT_ORIGINAL_FIGMA by its source. `corner.png` is the supplied Firefly 2 carpet corner. Fonts and rendering libraries are served locally.
