# Exhibition audio assets

## Ambience
- `palace-evening.ogg`: optimized offline Opus encoding (32 kbps) of the user-supplied audio **Palace Evening – Epic Mughal Instrumental Music Royal Darbar Vibes.mp3**. Full duration preserved (approximately 4m11s).
- Music copyright and redistribution rights **were not independently verified**. The repository owner should confirm permission before making a public release. The higher-bitrate original is retained in the conversation attachment, not bundled in the repository.
